What this is testing

This page exists because custom events are not visible on the current Vercel Hobby analytics plan. A separate page gives a simple free signal: visitors from the recovery checklist or issue records either reach this page or they do not.

Signal we are looking forIf visitors move from a recovery or issue page to this page, the alert offer is interesting enough to click. If they also send the email, the saved-stack alert idea has stronger demand.

Who this is for

This is for WooCommerce store owners, WordPress developers, and agencies responsible for checkout reliability, payment capture, refunds, webhooks, payouts, and order accounting.

Alert types

  • Checkout failures after plugin or gateway updates.
  • Successful payments stuck in Pending payment.
  • Gateway total and WooCommerce total mismatches.
  • Shipping or tax not collected correctly.
  • Refunds that do not match across systems.
  • Webhook, capture, or order-status update failures.
  • Payout, report, and accounting reconciliation issues.
