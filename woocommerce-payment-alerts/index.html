Why this page exists

The current SEO data does not prove that What Just Changed should become a broad content site. The strongest signal is narrower: WooCommerce payment and checkout risks. This page tests whether visitors want that narrow tracker as an alert product without requiring manual outreach.

What counts as signal: clicks on Request alerts, visits to this page from issue records, emails requesting alerts, or repeat visits to the payment issues hub.
